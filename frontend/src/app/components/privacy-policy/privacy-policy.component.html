Global & Indian Regulatory Commitment Statement

Sentinel Systems ("Sentinel", "we", "our", or "us") operates an enterprise Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform designed to store, manage, and exchange Electronic Health Information (EHI) under strict adherence to Indian national health policies and international data protection standards. This policy governs our compliance with India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Health Data Management Policy (HDMP), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), the Digital Information Security in Healthcare Act (DISHA), ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and HIPAA international baselines.

1. Scope & Multi-Model Governance

This policy applies to all clinical healthcare providers (Doctors, Nurses, Receptionists, Administrators, Auditors) and Patients accessing the Sentinel platform. Sentinel operates across two distinct operational models:

Model 1: Outpatient Clinic Appointments

Authorized on-duty clinical staff process appointment queues, conduct desk check-ins, record nurse triage vitals, document progress notes, issue eRx/labs, and finalize visit billing.

Model 2: Inpatient Hospitalization Care (ABAC)

Strict Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) enforces active care team assignment, ward department matching, and emergency break-glass logging for inpatient medical records.

2. Categories of Information Collected

Electronic Health Information (EHI)

  • ABDM ABHA Details: 14-digit ABHA ID & @abdm handle linking.
  • Master Patient Index (MPI): Demographics, MRN code, contact information.
  • Outpatient & Inpatient Records: Chief complaints, triage vitals, SOAP notes.
  • Coded Diagnoses: Problem lists mapped to ICD-10-CM & SNOMED CT.
  • eRx Pharmacy Orders: Prescriptions, dosages, routes, RxNorm codes.
  • Allergy Register: Allergen substances, severity ratings, food excipients.

System Audit & Technical Metadata

  • User Credentials: Hashed passwords, assigned RBAC/ABAC roles.
  • JWT Authentication Tokens: Claims, issuing timestamps, expirations.
  • Immutable Audit Trails: User ID, IP address, request URI, target entity ID.
  • HTTP Request Headers: User-Agent, Accept header (application/fhir+json).

3. Technical Security Safeguards (ISO 27001 & DISHA § 7)

Stateless JWT Bearer Token Security

Authentication utilizes JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with HMAC SHA-512 cryptographic keys. Tokens expire automatically after 24 hours to prevent unauthorized access from idle sessions.

Method-Level RBAC & ABAC Policy Controls

Every API endpoint is protected by Spring Security @PreAuthorize rules. Outpatient appointment queues are accessible by on-duty clinical staff, while inpatient records enforce strict ABAC care team relationship checks.

Smart Safety Engine & Clinical Decision Support

Prescription requests automatically trigger computerized drug-allergy contraindication checks against the patient's active allergy register prior to order confirmation.

4. Immutable WORM Audit Ledger (DISHA & ISO 27001)

Sentinel maintains a Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) audit ledger in the database (audit_logs). Any action involving patient data creation, view, update, export, or deletion generates an immutable audit record:

[AUDIT LOG ENTRY] Timestamp | User ID | Role | IP Address | Action (READ/WRITE/EXPORT) | Target Patient ID

5. HL7 FHIR R4 & ABDM Health Data Portability

In accordance with ABDM standards, DPDP Act 2023, and international open health data rules, Sentinel guarantees patient health data portability:

  • Patient $everything FHIR Bundle Export: Patients and authorized clinicians can export complete longitudinal records in standard application/fhir+json format.
  • ABDM ABHA Health Identifier Integration: Native support for 14-digit ABHA numbers and @abdm handles.
  • Standard Medical Terminology: Data is coded using international medical terminologies including LOINC (vitals/labs), SNOMED CT & ICD-10-CM (diagnoses), and RxNorm (eRx orders).

6. Patient Rights Under DPDP Act 2023 & International Norms

Right to Access & Export Request access or 1-click FHIR JSON downloads of your complete personal health record.
Right to Correction & Erasure Request corrections to inaccurate personal demographic details in accordance with DPDP Act § 12.
Consent & Audit Transparency Review explicit consent directives and obtain an accounting log of all system disclosures.
